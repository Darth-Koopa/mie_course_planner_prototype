Each course normally represents 0.5 FCE. Technical-course eligibility depends on the official MIE rules. ↗
2. Choose your study status Study status rules
Full-time: 12 months / 3 semesters standard; minimum 2 semesters; maximum 3 years. Recommended 3–4 courses per semester (1–5 allowed); maximum 10 courses per 3-semester academic year.
Extended full-time: 16–24 months standard; minimum 4 semesters; maximum 3 years. Recommended 3 courses per semester (1–5 allowed); maximum 7 courses per academic year.
Part-time: 28–36 months / 9 semesters standard; minimum 7 semesters; maximum 6 years. Recommended 1–2 courses per semester (1–2 allowed); maximum 4 courses per academic year.
Full-time and extended full-time require continuous registration. Switching and additional-fee conditions are included in the selected-status summary below. These are planning constraints, not a request to change your registration status. ↗
Used to calculate the maximum planning window. Earlier courses can still be recorded toward requirements, but do not extend the selected study-status duration.
